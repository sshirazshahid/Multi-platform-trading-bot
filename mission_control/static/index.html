    How to use Mission Control

    Telemetry panels read the bot's own state files. The Operations rail is the only mutating surface: 9 audited endpoints, each requiring your token, an operator note, and — for destructive actions — an exact confirm phrase. CONTROLLED_LIVE stays fail-closed behind the signed checklist.

    1. Overview — health at a glance. Mode and profile come from the bot's heartbeat (what the running process reports), not from .env intent.
    2. Positions — the open book with the bracket track (SL → entry → TP), plus the paper wallet vs its $5k per-venue seeds.
    3. Trade history — last 500 closed trades (a rolling cap), sortable, with the cumulative net-PnL curve and the win-rate band strip.
    4. Risk — daily PnL, drawdown, win/loss streak, incident latch.
    5. Shadow probes — research lanes and per-gate promotion verdicts. GATE_BLOCKED is the expected steady state; STARVED is the alarm.
    6. Why no trades — the skip reasons behind every candidate the bot rejected, over a window you choose.
    7. Brain — what it is thinking right now: the live decision cascade (scored → allowed → blocked → opened, counts that close because the two stages join on candidate_id), the binding constraint at each stage, reason strings decoded with the verbatim original kept alongside, and the research pipeline's own state. Probes there are log-only.
    8. Live gates — real-money path status. Blocked until you sign the checklist on disk yourself.
    9. Operations — Observe (reports), Operate (profile), Danger (restart, mode, kill switch, incident, CONTROLLED_LIVE).
