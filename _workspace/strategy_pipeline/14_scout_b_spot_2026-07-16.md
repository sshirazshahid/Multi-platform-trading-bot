## Candidate 1 — Stablecoin depeg micro-reversion on CEX spot (USDC/USDT, FDUSD/USDT)

**What it is.** When a major CEX-listed stablecoin trades a measurable discount to USDT on spot (threshold pre-registered, e.g. ≥20–30bps below 1.0000), buy the discounted stable with USDT; exit at peg restoration or a time stop. Long-discount only (the premium side is symmetric but smaller in-window). Pure spot, no leverage, no shorting.

**Mechanism (why someone pays).** Peg restoration is performed by primary-market arbitrageurs with mint/redeem access (Circle / First Digital); retail has no redemption access. When redemption is slow, gated, or impaired (banking hours, attestation panic, issuer disputes), the discount is the price of *immediacy* paid by exiting holders to whoever warehouses the claim until primary arbitrage catches up. That is compensated liquidity provision against a redemption constraint — a structural risk transfer, not a price-shape pattern.

**External evidence.**
- [arXiv 2601.18991, "Who Restores the Peg? A Mean-Field Game Approach" (Jan 2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.18991v1) — calibrated to the 2022/2023 USDT/USDC depegs; reproduces observed recovery half-lives; order-flow decomposition shows episodes with impaired primary redemption require **joint primary + secondary-market recovery** — i.e., secondary-market buyers (the retail-accessible leg) are structurally part of restoration. Recent, mechanism-grade.
- ["Stable Unstable: Evaluating Mean Reversion Profitability in Stablecoin Pair Depeg Events" (ResearchGate 374756033)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/374756033_Stable_Unstable_Evaluating_Mean_Reversion_Profitability_in_Stablecoin_Pair_Depeg_Events) — a directly on-point MR-profitability study. **Flags: 2023 (>12 months old); full text 403'd today — abstract/title-grade only.**
- ["Stablecoin devaluation risk", European Journal of Finance (2025)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1351847X.2025.2505757) — peer-reviewed 2025 treatment of deviation/devaluation dynamics.
- Measured tail event: **FDUSD 2025-04-02 depegged to $0.87 (−13%) on Justin Sun insolvency allegations, recovered to ~0.99 by next morning; First Digital honored ~$25.8M redemptions** ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/fdusd-depeg-sun-justin-insolvency-stablecoin), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/04/02/first-digital-to-pursue-legal-action-over-justin-sun-allegations-as-fdusd-drops), [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/justin-sun-stablecoin-fdusd-binance-depeg/)). Note this event is BEFORE our local cache window (starts 2025-05-31) and is n=1 — it is the tail-risk exhibit, not the edge claim.

**Data to test it.** LOCAL: `USDC-USDT_1h.parquet` + `FDUSD-USDT_1h.parquet` (1yr each, top-up ~6 weeks via the existing ohlcv harvester). The 1h-close deviation distribution is screenable immediately: count events ≥ threshold, reversion time, after-cost PnL per event. HONEST GAP: 1h closes understate intra-hour deviations, and wicks are not reliably fillable at 10s polling — a fresh 15m/1m keyless ccxt spot harvest (`exchange.fetch_ohlcv('USDC/USDT','1m')`, ~max 1000 bars/call, paginated) is required for fill-realism; the screen must fill at the NEXT bar's open after the trigger closes, never at the wick.

**Costs / feasibility @$420.** Spot, full notional, no leverage — no min-notional problem (Binance stable-pair min ≈ 5–10 USDT). Decisive cost fact: at the standard 10bps taker ×2 legs, only deviations >~25–30bps clear cost — a thin event set (in-window 1h max deviations were 44bps USDC / 59bps FDUSD). BUT Binance runs **standing zero-fee promotions on FDUSD and USDC spot pairs, updated into 2026** ([FDUSD zero-fee update, Jan 2026](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/01-15-2026-vip-fdusd-35107442260138); [USDC zero-fee promo page](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/91640281ce2541e18c5e6d80aa68c28e)) — if the traded pair is in the current zero-fee list, the cost floor drops to spread-only and the viable event set widens enormously. **Phase-2 precondition: pull live per-pair fees via `exchange.fetch_trading_fees()` and pre-register the actual fee, not the assumption.** BINDING RISK to state up front: this is structurally **short tail risk / negative skew** (selling insurance against issuer failure — a true insolvency turns a 30bps trade into a −13%+ full-notional loss, cf. FDUSD Apr-2025, USDC Mar-2023 $0.87). The frozen MC maxDD ≤0.25 capital-preservation gate is the binding test and must be run on full-notional exposure with an explicit issuer-failure stress scenario; the FDUSD leg is Binance-only (Bybit/Bitget don't list FDUSD/USDT meaningfully), USDC/USDT trades on all three venues.

**Novelty-vs-ledger: NEW.** No ledger row touches stablecoin pairs. Adjacency guard vs refuted RSI-MR: the trigger is an absolute peg-deviation threshold rooted in a redemption mechanism, never an oscillator or z-score of returns; if a screen drifts toward "price fell, buy", it stops.

## Candidate 2 — Delisting-announcement forced-flow (two sub-claims, legs flagged separately)

**What it is.** Binance (and Bybit/Bitget) publish delisting announcements with a removal date ~1–4 weeks out. Two pre-registerable sub-claims: **(2a) drift claim** — after the announcement-instant crash, does a measurable *continued* downward drift exist from (announcement + 1h) into the removal date, as deadline-forced holders exit? Shorting it requires the perp → **this leg is a futures claim, flagged as such** (and perps of delisting tokens are often delisted on their own schedule with settlement risk). **(2b) spot bounce claim** — on venues that KEEP the listing (Bybit/Bitget typically retain what Binance drops; ~65% of delisted tokens continue trading elsewhere), does the announcement-hour overshoot revert measurably, compensating buyers who absorb deadline-driven panic flow? This leg is pure spot, long-only, retail-expressible.

**Mechanism (why someone pays).** The announcement creates a hard, published deadline: Binance holders must sell or withdraw by removal date. That is calendar-scheduled, price-insensitive forced flow — the seller pays for immediacy, the absorber is compensated. Same mechanism family as the validated listing-short/unlock-short probes (public calendar → structural flow), opposite event.

**External evidence.** Measured per-event 2025 coverage (announcement-day moves): Apr-2025 batch — HARD −59%, NULS −48.3%, UFT −46.2%, VIDT −37.1% ([CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/price-prediction/binance-delisting-coins-after-vote-to-delist-sparks-price-crash)); FUN −27%, MDT −22%, FIO −20% ([BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/binance-delisting-six-altcoins-prices-crash/)); Sep-2025 — BAKE −25%, SLF −23%, HIFI −17% ([CCN](https://www.ccn.com/analysis/crypto/binance-to-delist-altcoins-prices-crash/)); anomaly FLM +19.7% post-announcement. Post-delisting: bid-ask spreads on surviving venues widen to 4–10% for illiquid names ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/binance-delistings-ripple-effect-altcoin-valuation-deep-dive-market-psychology-liquidity-risk-2601/)) — a real cost the bounce claim must eat. Academic: event-study evidence of significant negative abnormal returns on (−5,+10)-day windows around negative crypto events (2022, [ideas.repec](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ahs/journl/v7y2022i1p16-31.html) — old, family-support only); [Wiley APJFS 2025-26 delisting-prediction model, Binance case](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ajfs.70045). **No systematic study of the retail-tradeable post-announcement window was found — every 2025 number above is per-event news coverage (single-source-grade). "Insufficient external data; measurable via harvest" is the honest status.**

**Data to test it.** NOT local. Required harvest: (a) Binance delisting-announcement archive (public announcement pages carry timestamps; scrape title+timestamp for 2023–2026 — ~batches of 3–14 tokens, several per quarter); (b) OHLCV for delisted symbols — Binance's live klines API drops delisted pairs, but **data.binance.vision retains historical kline dumps for delisted symbols** (monthly zips, e.g. `https://data.binance.vision/data/spot/monthly/klines/<SYMBOL>USDT/1h/`); (c) surviving-venue leg via keyless ccxt on Bybit/Bitget (`fetch_ohlcv`, paginated) for the same tokens. Realized spreads on survivors are NOT in any local store — the screen must haircut the bounce leg by the documented 4–10% spread widening, or exclude names below a liquidity floor and count the exclusions.

**Costs / feasibility @$420.** Bounce leg (2b): spot long, full notional, fine at account size; the honest cost is the survivor-venue spread (dominates fees). Drift leg (2a): perp short = 2 fee legs + funding + perp-delisting settlement risk — **futures claim, and it should be routed/merged with scout C's futures scope if pursued.** Event frequency is adequate (2025 had ≥4 Binance batches → ~20–40 tokens/yr) but per-event independence must be policed (batch announcements are ONE market event, not N — the same pseudo-replication trap the audit flagged on unlock-short).

**Novelty-vs-ledger: NEW.** No ledger row covers delistings. Distinct from the in-shadow listing-short (opposite event, different calendar). Adjacency guard: entry trigger is the announcement timestamp + removal calendar, never a price-shape condition; if the bounce screen degenerates to "buy the big red candle" it re-enters refuted MR and stops.

## Candidate 3 — Staked-ETH wrapper discount capture (wBETH, stETH vs ETH on CEX spot)

**What it is.** wBETH and stETH trade on Binance spot against USDT. Each is redeemable for ETH (plus accrued staking yield) via unstaking queues (~2–5 days). Measure the deviation of the market ratio (WBETH/USDT ÷ ETH/USDT) below its slow-accruing fair trend; when the discount exceeds a pre-registered threshold, buy the wrapper and hedge with an equal-notional ETH perp short; exit on ratio normalization. **Flag: the hedge leg makes this a mixed spot+futures claim — the spot-only (unhedged) expression carries full ETH beta, which swamps a 30–100bps discount and is NOT proposed.**

**Mechanism (why someone pays).** The discount is the price of the redemption delay: sellers who cannot wait 2–5 days for the unstaking queue pay whoever warehouses the claim through it. Compensated time/liquidity risk transfer against a published redemption mechanism — same family logic as Candidate 1 with a longer constraint horizon.

**External evidence.** stETH typically trades within ~0.2% of ETH in 2025–26 with occasional wider slips flagged as arb entries ([blockchain.news flash coverage](https://blockchain.news/flashnews/steth-depeg-risk-recent-eth-steth-slip-triggers-arbitrage-and-signals-confidence-warning-for-long-term-stakers) — news-grade, single-source); historical stress benchmark: stETH at ~0.935 ETH during May-2022 (well-documented, but pre-window and pre-withdrawals-enabled — the redemption mechanism has structurally improved since, which cuts BOTH the risk and the expected discount width). **No measured 2025-26 study of CEX-spot wrapper-discount harvesting was found — insufficient external data; fully measurable locally.**

**Data to test it.** BEST LOCAL COVERAGE OF THE BATCH: `WBETH-USDT_1h` + `STETH-USDT_1h` + `ETH-USDT_1h` (1yr, local) give the full ratio series now; ETH perp funding for the hedge leg is in `funding_history/` on all three venues. The official wBETH/ETH conversion-rate series (Binance publishes it) is not local — a slow-trend fit (~+3%/yr accrual) is an acceptable pre-registered proxy, stated as such. **Recommended Phase-2 shape: a near-zero-cost descriptive pass FIRST — compute the 1yr deviation distribution; if p95 discount < (2 spot legs + 2 perp legs + expected hedge funding ≈ 25–40bps), STOP and record NO_GO without building the full screen.**

**Costs / feasibility @$420.** Four fee legs (wrapper buy/sell at 10bps-class spot taker; ETH perp open/close) + funding on the short hedge + wBETH/USDT book is thinner than ETH/USDT (spread haircut needed). Hedged pair at $420: two ~$100–200 legs are placeable (wBETH min notional ~10 USDT; ETH perp min ~5 USDT) inside the 12% exposure charter only at small absolute size — but per the owner's standing PAPER-mode guidance, the screen question is after-cost edge existence, not dollar yield. Honest expectation: **normal-regime deviations (<0.2%) do not clear four legs; the candidate lives or dies on the frequency of stress-widened discounts in the local window — likely NO_GO, cheaply provable.**

**Novelty-vs-ledger: NEW** (no ledger row on staked-asset pegs; not Kalman/cointegration pairs — the anchor is a redemption mechanism with a published fair value, not a fitted spread).

The residual announcement-second pop is a latency race this system loses at 10s polling. Zero candidates from this direction is the honest result.
- **Launchpool / HODLer-airdrop / earn-product calendar flow:** no measured 2025-26 study found; the tradeable proxy (BNB demand around snapshot windows) is confounded by BNB beta and burns; airdrop-distribution selling overlaps the in-shadow unlock-short family (distribution = supply event → scout C's adjacency, not a new spot claim).
- **Spot-perp basis dislocation outside funding settlement:** the spot expression ("buy spot when a perp-led cascade overshoots") is the same mechanism as the 07-11 Candidate 4 (post-cascade reversion) — already briefed, feasibility-limited by the absence of multi-year OI/liquidation history and intraday recovery speed. Not re-proposed; F1 owns the carry claim and settlement-window timing is a refuted ledger row (2026-07-11).
- **Index reconstitution:** no retail-relevant published-calendar flow into CEX spot books was found for 2025-26 (crypto index AUM flows route through OTC/APs, not lit CEX books). Insufficient data; nothing to screen.

## Ranking for edge-screener (Phase 2)

1. **Candidate 1 — stablecoin depeg micro-reversion.** Data local (1h now; 1m harvest cheap), mechanism recent and peer-reviewed-adjacent, and the Binance zero-fee promos are a possible step-change in viability that only a live fee pull can confirm. Binding test: negative-skew tail vs the frozen MC maxDD gate with an issuer-failure stress.
2. **Candidate 2 — delisting forced-flow.** Largest measured effect sizes (−17% to −59% per event) but per-event news-grade evidence and a real harvest project (announcement archive + data.binance.vision + survivor-venue OHLCV). Screen sub-claim 2b (spot bounce) first; 2a (drift short) is a futures claim to merge with scout C if pursued.
3. **Candidate 3 — staked-ETH wrapper discount.** Cheapest to falsify (all data local; descriptive pass ≈ an afternoon) but lowest prior: normal-regime deviations likely below the 4-leg cost floor. Run the descriptive pass; stop early on a cost-floor fail.
